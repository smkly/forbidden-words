Forbidden Words

Taboo for two, on one phone.

How it works

  1. The clue-giver holds the phone and describes the word without saying any red word.
  2. The other player guesses out loud.
  3. Tap "Got it" for each correct guess, "Taboo!" if a red word slips out, "Skip" to pass.
  4. When the timer ends, add the round to the score and pass the phone. First to the target wins.
